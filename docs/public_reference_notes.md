
# Public reference notes

Accessed: 2026-09-06

This repository is an **independent synthetic portfolio project**. It does not contain Vivo Energy or Engen internal data.

Public information used only to shape the business-domain model:

- Vivo Energy says it operates around **4,200 service stations across 29 markets**, sells about **21 billion litres of fuel per year**, has more than **2 billion litres of storage**, and serves around **2 million customers daily**.
  - https://www.vivoenergy.com/en/about-us
- Vivo Energy's public business model covers **Supply → Storage → Distribution → Retail → Commercial**.
  - https://www.vivoenergy.com/en/about-us/our-business-model
- Commercial sectors publicly referenced include **road transport, mining, construction, power, aviation and marine**, with LPG supplied to consumers and commercial customers.
  - https://www.vivoenergy.com/en/our-business/commercial
- Public retail information references fuel, lubricants, convenience retail, quick-service restaurants, car washes, banking/mobile services and LPG.
  - https://www.vivoenergy.com/en/our-business/retail
- Vivo Energy's strategy also references LPG, solar, EV charging and new mobility.
  - https://www.vivoenergy.com/en/about-us/strategy
- Engen public material in 2025/2026 references **1,000+ / 1,050+ service stations in South Africa**.
  - https://www.engen.co.za/media/media-release/engen-celebrates-grand-opening-flagship-site-sandton-and-marks-100th-woolworths-foodstop
- Older Engen public material listed South African fuel terminal/depot locations such as Alrode, Langlaagte, Waltloo, Wentworth, Ladysmith, Montague Gardens, Mossel Bay, Bethlehem, Bloemfontein, Kroonstad, East London, Gqeberha/Port Elizabeth, Klerksdorp, Rustenburg, Vryburg, Hoedspruit, Makhado, Mokopane, Kimberley, Upington, Mbombela/Nelspruit, Secunda and eMalahleni.
  - These are used only as **public/legacy location seeds**. Current operational status must be independently validated.

## Important modelling rule

All station names, customer names, transaction values, employee names, asset identifiers, GPS points, sales, prices, margins, incidents and operational metrics generated by this project are synthetic.

Coordinates for synthetic service stations are randomly jittered around real South African towns/cities and should **not** be interpreted as actual Engen/Vivo Energy site coordinates.
